# Reference adaptation

The funky experience follows the user-requested Pixel Melbourne reference. The current brief adds an original split-world entry and a separate classic experience with the same portfolio data.

Palette: coral #ff5354 (mobile #fe494a), pink #ffb5d2, electric blue #0800ff, lime #e4fe52, nude #e0c8bb, brown #200e02.

Typography: Swell for the wordmark, display titles, skill words and footer; Acumin for controls and prose.

Structure: red animated wordmark and three-line statement → nude pinned 3D carousel with seven categories → blue introduction and playful seven-item word garden → pink and blue Let’s Talk footer. The mobile logo repeats three times, as in the reference. Menus, contact details and category copy are adapted to Mahdi.

All headline and brand substitutions follow the user’s explicit instructions. New slide art is temporary, generated and openly identified in category dialogs. No fictional employment, project outcomes or credentials are added.

Interaction fidelity: replace the earlier skewing text with projected Swell glyph contours, using staggered 3D rotations, wireframe extrusion, a solid hold and reverse exit on a 5.94-second loop. Keep the existing coral/pink wordmark palette and three mobile repetitions. Match the reference sidebar’s 560px desktop width, rounded left corners, coral/lime/pink entrance layers, lime links, hover fills and bottom contact columns; on mobile it covers the viewport. Rotate hand stickers 25 degrees or briefly scale cursor/heart stickers to 1.2 on hover. Use Matter.js for falling decorative visuals, real collisions and pointer dragging, with an explicit restore control.

## Split-world entry and classic direction

Entry: two complete stylized 3D worlds separated by a luminous jagged crack. The fox and pianist share rendering quality while their environments, clothing, typography and controls differ. Keep faces visible above the bottom-aligned choice titles and buttons. Desktop panels sit side by side; mobile stacks them while keeping both choices visible in the viewport. Mouse parallax changes perspective and lighting gently. Entry buttons use the exact text “I’m funky” and “I’m classic”. Every reload returns here. No persistent preference, URL mode or switch-style button.

Classic tokens: navy #0c1023, violet-black #151127, silver #eeedf6, muted silver #b4b3ca, soft violet #b49bff, glass borders #e5dcff24. Gloock carries composed display type; Acumin carries quiet controls and readable prose.

Classic structure: asymmetric typographic hero beside a live layered glass monogram → seven discipline previews with alternating wide/narrow proportions → personal practice and frosted tool panel → spacious contact invitation. Glass has edge highlights, depth, restrained blur and violet reflections. Motion answers pointer position through light and panel tilt. Scrolling opens planes and shutters, resolves focus, reveals text horizontally, and brings in the final contact through a light effect. Reduced motion immediately displays all content.

Review against the brief: the entry is anchored in the requested fox/piano worlds, and the classic hero uses design plates rather than a generic dashboard card. Keep the user's glass/gradient direction, but vary the seven work proportions and the reveal choreography so the page does not become a repeated uniform grid. The pianist is a fictional visual mascot, not an assertion that Mahdi is a musician or a portrait of him.
