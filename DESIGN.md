## Split-world entry and classic direction

Entry: two complete stylized 3D worlds separated by a luminous jagged crack. The fox and pianist share rendering quality while their environments, clothing, typography and controls differ. Keep faces visible above the bottom-aligned choice titles and buttons. Desktop panels sit side by side; mobile stacks them while keeping both choices visible in the viewport. Mouse parallax changes perspective and lighting gently. Entry buttons use the exact text “I’m funky” and “I’m classic”. Every reload returns here. No persistent preference, URL mode or switch-style button.

Classic tokens: navy #0c1023, violet-black #151127, silver #eeedf6, muted silver #b4b3ca, soft violet #b49bff, glass borders #e5dcff24. Gloock carries composed display type; Acumin carries quiet controls and readable prose.

Classic structure: asymmetric typographic hero beside a live layered glass monogram → seven discipline previews with alternating wide/narrow proportions → personal practice and frosted tool panel → spacious contact invitation. Glass has edge highlights, depth, restrained blur and violet reflections. Motion answers pointer position through light and panel tilt. All seven work cards use the same plane reveal as Youtube Thumbnails, with matching tilt, opacity, travel and easing. The other sections resolve focus, reveal text horizontally, and bring in the final contact through a light effect. Each reveal follows a 34vh scroll range, using native scroll timelines when supported and paused Web Animations in the frame-based fallback. Moving up reverses its plane/focus/ink/light choreography; moving down rebuilds it. Geometry is measured from untransformed layout positions, including after font loading or responsive changes. Reduced motion immediately displays all content.

The tool panel lists Photoshop, Illustrator, Premier, After effects, Ui/Ux and Ai Tools. Keep the small rounded glass letter icons. Colour overlays blend into blue, orange, two distinct violets, teal and red according to pointer proximity within 60px. On touch devices, each icon lights as its centre moves from 78% to 56% of the viewport, and dims in the same order when scrolling back. The contact buttons blend into Telegram blue and Instagram pink while hovered, focused or touched, and restore on pointer release or cancellation.

Review against the brief: the entry is anchored in the requested fox/piano worlds, and the classic hero uses design plates rather than a generic dashboard card. Keep the user's glass/gradient direction, and preserve the seven work proportions while using the same reveal choreography for every work card, as requested. The pianist is a fictional visual mascot, not an assertion that Mahdi is a musician or a portrait of him.

The classic monogram stays visibly glass even when unlit: its letter, glass illumination, halo, ring and orb share one scroll-controlled light. The M has its own clear space above “Form. Feeling.” so the two no longer overlap. It starts off, lights as the composition centre moves from 85% to 52% of the viewport, and dims through the identical curve on scrolling upward. When the monogram is already visible at entry, it still starts off and uses at least 18vh of scroll for its light range. It remains lit below the hero instead of resetting offscreen. Pointer movement only retains its existing tilt, without overriding the light. The composition itself is not hidden by a separate reveal. Lighting uses cached layout geometry and the existing frame scheduler, with a gentle-start 900ms CSS opacity transition and an immediate reduced-motion path. Funky carousel captions fade in and out over 600ms with ease-in-out; the first caption waits until the carousel approaches the viewport centre, and subsequent captions follow their active card; the ring, scroll ranges and controls retain their existing behavior.
